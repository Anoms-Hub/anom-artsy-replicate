## Content Platform Strategy (From Master Platform Guide)

Substack is the main platform hub. Instagram and Facebook support distribution. Spreadshop is the product layer.

Content workflow: Create idea → Build full post on Substack first → Adapt outward for Instagram/Facebook → Evaluate merch potential → Publish in layers → Archive intentionally.

Archive lanes: Identity | Process | Visual Storytelling | AI + Art | Profile/Presence Tips | AO Brand/Studio Notes | Featured Work and Merch Story Posts
